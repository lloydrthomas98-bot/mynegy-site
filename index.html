How it works

Write today’s response. Open one fresh writing challenge each day and answer it in your own words.

Get coaching on your writing. Mynegy scores your response, marks specific lines and gives you practical goals for next time.

Train beyond the daily challenge. Use Reading Coach for spoken delivery, rehearse conversations in Roleplay Studio, or practise focused skills in the learning library.

See your progress build. Review past feedback, strengthen your Communication DNA, keep your streak and share daily responses with the community.
