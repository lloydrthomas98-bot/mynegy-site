Privacy Policy

Last updated: 8 August 2026

Who we are

Mynegy is built and operated by Mynegy Ltd, a company registered in England and Wales (company number 17395048, registered office: 8 Heol-y-beddau, Caerffili, CF83 1AH) (“we”, “us”, “our”). Mynegy Ltd is the data controller for your personal data. This policy explains what data we collect, why, and your rights. Questions: support@mynegy.com.

Data we collect

Account details (email, display name); the text responses, voice recordings and transcripts you submit for AI feedback; usage and progress data (streaks, scores, Communication DNA, levels); and, if you opt in, content you share to the community. Voice recordings are stored only as long as needed to produce your analysis.

How we use it

To provide AI coaching and feedback, track your progress, operate community features you opt into, moderate shared content, process subscriptions, and improve the app.

Legal basis (UK/EU users)

We process your data to perform our contract with you (providing the service), on the basis of your consent (for example, sharing to the community or optional analytics), and for our legitimate interests in running and improving Mynegy. You can withdraw consent at any time.

Aggregated & anonymised data

We may create aggregated and anonymised datasets and statistics from app usage — for example, overall trends in how people communicate. This data cannot be used to identify you. We reserve the right to use, publish, share and sell such aggregated and anonymised data for any purpose, including commercial ones. We do not sell data that can identify you as an individual.

Processors we use

Supabase (authentication, database, storage), OpenAI (AI analysis and moderation of text and audio you submit), RevenueCat and the App Store / Google Play (subscription processing), Google AdMob (non-personalised advertising for free users), and optional product analytics (PostHog, EU hosting) if you enable it. These providers process data under their own security terms.

Community content

If you share a response, it becomes visible to other users (with your name or anonymously as you choose). Shared content is screened by automated moderation and can be reported. You can delete your shares and block other users at any time.

Your rights

You can export your data or permanently delete your account from within the app’s Settings. Depending on your location you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or restrict processing of your data, and to object to certain processing. To exercise these rights, contact support@mynegy.com. UK users may also complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office (ico.org.uk).

Retention & security

We keep your data while your account is active and delete it on request. We use industry standard safeguards, but no system is perfectly secure.

Children

Mynegy is directed at users aged 16 and over in the United Kingdom. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 16. If you believe a child has created an account, contact support@mynegy.com and we will delete it promptly. Age is confirmed at sign-up.

Optional analytics

Product analytics (PostHog, EU hosting) are off by default. If you enable them in Settings we process limited usage events to improve the app. You can turn this off at any time. We do not sell personal data.

Advertising

Free users may see non-personalised interstitial advertisements provided by Google AdMob; Premium is ad-free. Before requesting ads, Google’s consent tools are shown where required. The advertising SDK may process an IP-derived approximate location, device or app identifiers, ads viewed and interactions, product interactions, crash logs and performance data to deliver, measure, secure and improve ads. Mynegy does not request Apple’s tracking permission or use IDFA, and we do not sell your personal data. You can revisit available choices in Settings.

AI processing

Text and voice you submit for coaching are sent to our AI processor (currently OpenAI) to generate feedback and moderation signals. Do not submit special-category data you are not comfortable sharing. AI output is automated and may be imperfect.

Changes

We may update this policy. Material changes will be notified in-app or by email, and the “last updated” date above will change.
